# Merlin notes

- Don't make it obvious you know who is evil. Both lessons show the assassin picking out Merlin from a perfect voting and naming record.
- Don't repeatedly nominate yourself for teams, and don't always oppose exactly the teams that contain evil players. Sometimes approve a borderline team, and let others lead the opposition.
- Avoid confident, repeated naming of suspects. Voice doubt more tentatively, and spread some suspicion onto a player you know nothing about, so the assassin has other plausible Merlin candidates.
