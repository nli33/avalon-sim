# Morgana notes

- Don't be transparent early: a player who steers teams away from the evil players and rejects evil teams from the start looks like Merlin. Hide the evil pair by not pushing too obviously against or for your partner. When you're the one doing that kind of accurate targeting, expect the assassin's attention.
- If you join a small team and play fail, don't claim success while the other members can deny it. As the only unproven member you become the obvious suspect. Plan a blame story that points at a teammate, or consider whether a fail is worth the exposure.
- When picking the assassination target, favor the player who consistently and accurately opposed the evil teams and named the evil players early.
