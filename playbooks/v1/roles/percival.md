## Percival notes

**Telling the two candidates apart (Merlin vs Morgana)**
- Watch behavior, not just votes. The candidate who steers toward clean teams, opposes teams that later turn out to include a failer, and keeps pushing teams that pass is probably Merlin.
- Don't read "pushing themselves onto teams" as evil by default. It can be Merlin steering away from evil.
- If a quest fails and the only people on it are one candidate and one other player, the other candidate is likely Merlin (the failer is likely the candidate on that team).

**Protecting Merlin**
- Once one candidate looks like Merlin, don't point at them publicly or get the table to confirm them. The evil assassin can read the same signals.

**Gathering information**
- Proposing or approving a minimal team that includes me (I know I'm good) is a sound way to learn. Quests that succeed with me on them clear my teammates, which narrows the evil set.
