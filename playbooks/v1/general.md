## Notes (from Game 1 lessons)

**Reading others**
- A player who repeatedly pushes for the same specific partner on team after team looks linked to that partner. Treat a pusher/defender pair as suspect, especially after a failed quest.
- After a failed quest, a player who blames the team members and pushes to get a partner onto the next team is suspect.
- A lone early reject with vague reasons is weak evidence of evil. Early confident opposition to a team may come from a player with hidden knowledge. Judge it against who later turns out to be on failing teams.

**Protecting the informed good player**
- If a player's reads are consistently accurate (spotting evil, pushing clean teams), the assassin will likely target them once good nears 3 wins. Avoid openly crowning or over-endorsing that player.
- Winning quests with a team known to be clean is correct. Disguise how you knew.

**As evil**
- Don't openly defend your partner or push for them team after team. It links the evil pair.
